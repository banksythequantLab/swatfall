SWATFALL

One hand steers. One hand fires. Both hands swat.

Controls

Quest (hands, seated): slide your left hand left/right to steer the emitter · pinch (or finger-gun thumb-drop) with your right hand to fire · when a glowmoth breaks formation and flies at you, swat it with either hand · hold a palm up to pause · poke the floating panels to choose. Swap hands on the panel.

Desktop: mouse/←→ steer · click/Space fire · click a diver to swat · P pause.

Controllers: move the left controller to steer, right trigger fires, poke panels with the controller tip, Y/B pauses.
